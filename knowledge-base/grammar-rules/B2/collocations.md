# B2_ADV: Advanced collocations

Collocations are combinations of words that occur naturally together. English commonly says **make a decision**, **do homework**, **take responsibility**, **pay attention**, and **make progress**. The choice of verb is often conventional, so a word-for-word translation from Spanish may be understandable but unnatural.

Learn a collocation with a short context rather than treating one word as having a single universal translation. For example, **do** appears in “do homework” but **make** appears in “make a mistake”; many verbs also combine naturally with other nouns. Adjust the tense of the verb while retaining the natural combination: “made a decision”, “has made progress”.

- ❌ “We did a decision.” → ✅ “We made a decision.” — Tomamos una decisión.
- ❌ “She made her homework.” → ✅ “She did her homework.” — Ella hizo sus deberes.
- ❌ “Please make attention.” → ✅ “Please pay attention.” — Por favor, presta atención.
- ❌ “He took a mistake.” → ✅ “He made a mistake.” — Cometió un error.

Spanish **hacer** may correspond to **make** or **do** in different expressions. The noun and context help determine the natural English verb.
