# A2_INIT: Regular and irregular past simple

Use the past simple for a finished event or state in a finished past time: “We visited Lima last year.” Regular affirmative verbs usually add **-ed**: “work, worked”; a consonant plus **y** often changes to **-ied**: “study, studied”. Some short verbs double a final consonant: “stop, stopped”. Irregular affirmative verbs have individual past forms, such as **go/went**, **see/saw**, and **have/had**; learn these forms with their base verbs.

For most questions and negatives use **did** or **didn't** plus the base verb, whether the verb is regular or irregular: “Did you go?” and “I didn't work.” Do not use a past form after **did**. The past of **be** is **was/were**, without **did** in ordinary questions: “Were they late?”

- ❌ “She studyed yesterday.” → ✅ “She studied yesterday.” — Ella estudió ayer.
- ❌ “We goed home.” → ✅ “We went home.” — Fuimos a casa.
- ❌ “Did he saw it?” → ✅ “Did he see it?” — ¿Lo vio?
- ❌ “They didn't went.” → ✅ “They didn't go.” — No fueron.

Spanish may express past time with a different choice of tense. Identify whether the English context describes a completed event before selecting the past simple.
