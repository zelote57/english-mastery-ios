# B1_INT: Passive voice in present and past simple

Use the passive when the receiver of an action is the grammatical subject or when the person who performs it is unknown or less important. Form it with **be + past participle**. In the present simple use **am/is/are**: “The room is cleaned every day.” In the past simple use **was/were**: “The room was cleaned yesterday.”

Add **by** when identifying the agent is useful: “The novel was written by a local author.” The form of **be** agrees with the new subject, and the main verb is a past participle. Irregular verbs may have a participle different from their past simple: “write, wrote, written.”

- ❌ “The room is clean every day by staff.” → ✅ “The room is cleaned every day by staff.” — El personal limpia la habitación todos los días.
- ❌ “The letters was sent yesterday.” → ✅ “The letters were sent yesterday.” — Las cartas se enviaron ayer.
- ❌ “The book was wrote by Ana.” → ✅ “The book was written by Ana.” — El libro fue escrito por Ana.

Spanish can use **se** to express an impersonal or passive meaning. Translate the intended meaning rather than copying **se** into the English sentence.
