# A2_INIT: Basic time expressions

Use **yesterday**, **last week/month/year**, and **two days ago** to locate an event in a finished past period. These expressions commonly accompany the past simple: “I called yesterday” or “They left two hours ago.” Put the duration before **ago**, not after it: “three years ago”. Use **last** directly before a time noun, without **the**, in expressions such as “last Monday”.

In English, **ago** looks backward from now. Spanish often expresses the same idea with **hace** before the duration, so translating the word order directly can be misleading. A time expression can appear at the beginning or end of a sentence; a comma is common after a longer introductory expression.

- ❌ “I arrived ago two days.” → ✅ “I arrived two days ago.” — Llegué hace dos días.
- ❌ “We met the last week.” → ✅ “We met last week.” — Nos conocimos la semana pasada.
- ❌ “She visits us yesterday.” → ✅ “She visited us yesterday.” — Ella nos visitó ayer.

Use **in 2020** for a named year and **on Monday** for a day. Choose the verb tense from the whole time context.
