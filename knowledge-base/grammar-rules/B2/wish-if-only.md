# B2_INIT: wish / if only

Use **wish** or **if only** with a past form to express that a present situation were different: “I wish I had more time.” Use the past perfect to express regret about a past situation: “If only I had left earlier.” These past forms describe unreal meaning, not necessarily past time.

Use **wish + would + base verb** for a desired change, often another person's behaviour or an external situation: “I wish the rain would stop.” It is not the usual pattern for a present state controlled by the speaker; “I wish I knew” expresses the state. **If only** often adds emphasis, but the grammar is similar.

- ❌ “I wish I have more time.” → ✅ “I wish I had more time.” — Ojalá tuviera más tiempo.
- ❌ “If only I knew about it yesterday,” when expressing past regret → ✅ “If only I had known about it yesterday.” — Ojalá lo hubiera sabido ayer.
- ❌ “I wish it would stops.” → ✅ “I wish it would stop.” — Ojalá dejara de llover.

Spanish **ojalá** can translate several English patterns. Identify whether the wish concerns the present, a past regret, or a desired change before choosing the English form.
