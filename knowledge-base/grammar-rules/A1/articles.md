# A1: Articles (a/an/the)

Use **a** or **an** with a singular countable noun when mentioning one non-specific person or thing. The choice depends on sound: use **an** before a vowel sound (“an apple”, “an hour”) and **a** before a consonant sound (“a book”, “a university”). A silent written letter does not determine the sound.

Use **the** when the listener can identify the particular person or thing, for example after it has already been mentioned. English also uses **a/an** for singular occupations, where Spanish often has no article. Do not add a singular indefinite article to a plural noun.

- ❌ “She is teacher.” → ✅ “She is a teacher.” — Ella es profesora.
- ❌ “It is a apple.” → ✅ “It is an apple.” — Es una manzana.
- ❌ “I saw a dog. A dog was friendly.” → ✅ “I saw a dog. The dog was friendly.” — Vi un perro. El perro era amistoso.
- ❌ “They are a students.” → ✅ “They are students.” — Son estudiantes.

Articles depend on meaning and context. **The** can also be used for a specific plural noun: “the students in this room”.
